# EchoBound: Riftborn — Game Design

## Core fantasy
The player is a forgotten hero whose memories were split into five Echoes. Each region reveals part of the truth.

## Session loop
1. Fight actively.
2. Spend energy on Rift Burst.
3. Earn gold and XP.
4. Upgrade hero.
5. Unlock cosmetics and heroes.
6. Push to a new region.
7. Leave the game; Auto Hunt and offline income continue.
8. Return to collect progress and chase the next story beat.

## Long-term loop
Region → chapter boss → Echo Key → new region → new hero/outfit → harder enemies → final Echo.

## Monetization philosophy
Avoid pay-to-win as the primary design. Cosmetics, optional convenience and clearly disclosed rewarded ads can be considered after the free progression loop is satisfying.

## Content targets for 1.0
- 5 regions
- 20+ normal enemies
- 10+ bosses
- 4–6 heroes
- 20+ outfits
- 50+ quests
- 5 story chapters
- 30+ achievements
- 3 rotating events
